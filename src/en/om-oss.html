About us

Technology is our job. Your business is our focus.

Norden Media UF builds websites and live streams events. We are students at Björknäsgymnasiet in Boden and run the business as a student company in partnership with Ung Företagsamhet (Junior Achievement Sweden). We take care of the tech, so that our clients can spend their time on their business.

We build websites that deliver results and stream events such as the business breakfasts in Boden and Boden Municipal Council meetings. Because we are based here, we are always close by and happy to meet in person.

Long-term

Your website stays after the UF year.

A UF student company runs for one school year, but working with us does not have to end there. Our plan is to continue the business as a limited company, with the same people and the same service, for every client who wants to stay.

The website and all its content belong to you. If you would rather run it yourselves, we show you how everything works and hand over all the material. If you need changes later on, we are still here to help at an hourly rate.
