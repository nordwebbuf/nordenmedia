From the first call to ongoing operations.

You tell us what you need, and we take care of the rest. It usually takes three to six weeks from the first call to launch. How fast it goes depends on the size of your website and how quickly we receive material from you. After launch we stay on and look after everything.

Book a call

  1. First call

    We get to know your business, your customers and how you work today. Then we know what the website needs to contain and what will make the biggest difference.

  2. Proposal

    You get a fixed price for the build and a clear monthly fee, with what is included. Based on what you want, you get a short form about the content to fill in. After launch you can always change all the content yourselves, easily through your customer portal.

  3. Design and development

    We write the copy, build the website on a test address that only you can see and connect the systems. You can follow the work all the way.

  4. Approval and launch

    You review and approve the website. Then we move your web address and email and publish it, so you show up on Google from day one.

  5. Ongoing operations

    We keep the website running, secure and up to date, and help you whenever something needs changing. With the Sarek and Kebne packages you also get a monthly report with suggested improvements.

Long-term

Your website stays after the UF year.

A UF student company runs for one school year, but working with us does not have to end there. Our plan is to continue the business as a limited company, with the same people and the same service, for every client who wants to stay.

The website and all its content belong to you. If you would rather run it yourselves, we show you how everything works and hand over all the material. If you need changes later on, we are still here to help at an hourly rate.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost?

You pay a fixed start-up cost for the build itself, from SEK 5,495, then a monthly fee from SEK 195 for hosting, updates and support. As a UF student company we do not charge VAT, so the prices are what you pay. Live streaming is quoted per event. See all prices.

When do we pay?

Only when the website is finished and you are happy with it. The start-up cost is invoiced once you have approved the finished website, and the monthly fee starts when the website goes live.

What happens when the UF year ends?

Our plan is to continue the business as a limited company, with the same people and the same service, for the clients who want to stay. The website and its content belong to you. If you would rather run it yourselves, we show you how everything works and hand over all the material, and if you need changes later on we help at an hourly rate.

How long does it take?

Usually three to six weeks from the first call to launch, depending on the size of the website and how quickly we receive material from you. See how it works.

Do we need to know anything about tech?

No. You tell us what you want and we handle the rest. Everything is explained in plain language.

We have no website at all. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We start from scratch and take care of everything, from domain and email to a finished website that shows up on Google.

Can you take over our old website, domain and email?

Yes. We move your domain, DNS and email for you and plan the move so that you lose neither email nor your rankings on Google.

What does optimisation for AI answers mean?

More and more people search using AI services such as ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews. We structure the content on your website so that these services understand what you offer and can point customers to you.
